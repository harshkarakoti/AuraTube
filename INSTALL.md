# How to Install AuraTube

AuraTube is an open-source, client-side ambient lighting extension for YouTube.

---

### Step-by-Step Installation Guide

#### Step 1: Download & Extract
1. Download `auratube-extension.zip`.
2. Right-click the `.zip` file and select **Extract All...** (or unzip it to any folder on your computer).

#### Step 2: Open Extensions in Your Browser
Open your browser and navigate to the extensions management page:
* **Google Chrome**: [`chrome://extensions`](chrome://extensions)
* **Brave Browser**: [`brave://extensions`](brave://extensions)
* **Microsoft Edge**: [`edge://extensions`](edge://extensions)
* **Opera**: [`opera://extensions`](opera://extensions)

#### Step 3: Enable Developer Mode
In the top-right corner of the Extensions page, toggle **Developer mode** to **ON**.

#### Step 4: Load the Unpacked Extension
1. In the top-left corner, click **Load unpacked**.
2. Select the folder where you extracted `auratube-extension.zip` (the folder containing `manifest.json`).
3. Click **Select Folder**.

#### Step 5: Test on YouTube
1. Open or refresh any video on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com).
2. The ambient lighting effect will immediately glow around the video player!
3. To customize blur, spread, edge sizing, and presets, click the AuraTube settings icon directly on the video player controls.

---

### Updating to New Versions
When a new version is released:
1. Replace the files in your existing AuraTube folder with the new files.
2. Go back to `chrome://extensions` and click the **Reload (⟳)** button on the AuraTube card.
