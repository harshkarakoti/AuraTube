AuraTube for YouTube

Transform your YouTube watching experience with dynamic, real-time ambient lighting effects that glow beyond the edges of the video player. No extra hardware required—everything runs smoothly in your browser using hardware acceleration!

✨ Core Features:
1. Dynamic Ambient Light:
   - Ultra-smooth blur & spread customization
   - Cinematic edge sizing and fade curves
   - Vibrant color enhancement & contrast filters

2. Intelligent Black Bar Detection:
   - Auto-detect and remove letterboxes (top/bottom) and pillarboxes (sides)
   - Eliminates dead space so the ambient glow perfectly hugs the action

3. High Performance & Efficiency:
   - GPU-accelerated with WebGL
   - Smart Battery Saver mode for static videos and music tracks
   - Customizable framerate limiter & synchronization

4. Sleek Controls:
   - Quick in-player settings menu right on the YouTube controls bar
   - Instant live preview of adjustments
   - Clean, modern cyberpunk/violet neon interface

5. Privacy Focused:
   - 100% client-side operation
   - Zero telemetry, no external trackers, no personal data collection
