# Privacy Policy — AuraTube

AuraTube respects your privacy:
- AuraTube operates strictly client-side on YouTube video pages (`https://www.youtube.com/*`).
- No browsing history, video identifiers, personal credentials, or analytics are collected, stored, or transmitted to any remote servers or third parties.
- All configuration settings (such as blur, brightness, edge sizes, and spread) are stored locally in your browser's local storage and remain entirely on your device.
